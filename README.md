# Walking Trex

A 3D-printed bipedal dinosaur robot that learns to walk in simulation and runs the learned policy on its own Raspberry Pi.

- **Robot:** 8 Hiwonder LX-16A serial bus servos (head, tail, hip / femur / tibia per leg), passive 4-bar ankles, a BNO055 IMU and a Raspberry Pi 4.
- **Training:** PPO in Isaac Sim 6.0.1, with an Isaac Lab 3.0 port of the same task that is bit-identical. The actor only sees what the robot can measure.
- **Servo model:** fitted to the real servos, then refit to the robot's own walking logs (1.4° RMS on the floor).
- **Before hardware:** every policy is also checked in MuJoCo through the exact control loop the Pi runs.

## Status (2026-10-21)

- **On the robot:** two policies have walked on the real floor at a 0.10 m/s command.
  - v9m: 0.41 m in 10 s (41% of the command), straight.
  - v9n: 0.25 m in 10 s (25%), turning 49° from a learned one-sided posture.
- **Next:** v9o (v9n + mirror loss; posture centred, no turn in MuJoCo) waits for its floor test.
- **Main open problem:** the gap between simulated and real speed. Every run, with its change, targets and results, is in [docs/ABLATION_STUDY.md](docs/ABLATION_STUDY.md).

## Quick start

```bash
export OMNI_KIT_ACCEPT_EULA=YES
PY=~/miniconda3/envs/isaac6/bin/python      # Isaac Sim stack
LAB=~/miniconda3/envs/isaaclab6/bin/python  # Isaac Lab, MuJoCo, fitting

walk_rl/run_experiment.sh v9o walk_rl/experiments/v9o_mirror_loss.yaml v9m v9n   # train + full evaluation (~2 h)
$LAB pi_runner/render_mujoco.py --run v9o --tag speeds                          # MuJoCo video in outputs/videos/
python3 walk_rl/ablation_study.py                                               # regenerate docs/ABLATION_STUDY.md
pi_runner/deploy.sh pi@trex.local v9o                                     # to the robot
```
